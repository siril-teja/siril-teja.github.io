I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at [MDsim S.A.](https://mdsim.health) in Luxembourg, where I work on the biomechanical verification and clinical validation of [SPINEsim](https://mdsim.health) — a patient-specific digital twin platform for spine surgical planning. This position is funded by the [FNR (Fonds National de la Recherche)](https://www.fnr.lu), Luxembourg's national research fund. I collaborate closely with [Prof. Hans-Joachim Wilke](https://www.uni-ulm.de/med/institut-fuer-unfallchirurgische-forschung-und-biomechanik/forschungsfelder/biomechanik-der-wirbelsaeule/) at the Institute of Orthopaedic Research and Biomechanics, University of Ulm, Germany.

I completed my PhD in Mechanical Engineering (Biomechanics) at [McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca), working at the [Musculoskeletal Biomechanics Research Lab](https://www.mcgill.ca/mbr) under the supervision of [Prof. Mark Driscoll](https://www.mcgill.ca/mecheng/people/staff/mark-driscoll). My doctoral research centered on developing and validating a robotic benchtop spinal model, 3D-printable analogue spine surrogates, and a high-fidelity MATLAB rigid-body spine model inclusive of intra-abdominal pressure — all aimed at advancing research in spinal biomechanics, low back pain, and spinal stability.

My broader research interests span **musculoskeletal modeling**, **robotics and control**, and **experimental validation**, with translational applications in digital health and medical device development. I am particularly interested in bridging the gap between computational models and clinical practice.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering from [Manipal University](https://manipal.edu/mu.html), India, and completed my bachelor's thesis with the [R&MM Research Group](https://www.brubotics.eu/research/self-healing-research) at Vrije Universiteit Brussel, Belgium, where I designed and prototyped a self-healing soft robotic gripper.

Previously, I was part of [Mars Society South Asia](https://southasia.marssociety.org/), promoting space exploration and STEM education by organizing robotics competitions such as [IRC](https://southasia.marssociety.org/indianroverchallenge/) and [IRDC](https://southasia.marssociety.org/irdc/).

In my free time, I enjoy [photography](https://www.instagram.com/siril.td/), [cycling](https://www.strava.com/athletes/82563036), and going on long-distance biking trips during the summer.
